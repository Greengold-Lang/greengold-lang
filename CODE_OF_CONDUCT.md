# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to work on a programming language together. To keep it that way:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask questions before you assume a mistake was careless.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, email **founder@greengoldlang.com**. Reports are handled privately.

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to people representing the project in public.
